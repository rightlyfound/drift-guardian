# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include
respectful communication, accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on
what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
trolling, public or private abuse, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers at
conduct@rightlyfound.com. Maintainers will review reports fairly and privately.
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
